# Privacy Policy for GIFforge

**Last updated:** October 2026

At **GIFforge**, we place utmost importance on your personal and organizational privacy. This Privacy Policy details how the GIFforge browser extensions and related tools handle data.

---

### 1. Zero-Data Collection Principle
GIFforge does **not** collect, store, transmit, or monetize any personal information, usage metrics, device fingerprints, or analytical telemetry.

### 2. Screen & Video Data Handling
- **100% In-Browser Execution:** When you record your screen or upload a video file into GIFforge, the entire frame parsing, computer vision scoring, palette computation, and GIF compilation take place strictly on your local computer using client-side JavaScript, WebAssembly, and HTML5 Canvas APIs.
- **No Cloud Transmission:** At no point is video or image data sent across any network, cloud server, or external service.

### 3. Extension Permissions
The browser extension requests only the minimum permissions required to provide functionality:
- `storage`: Used solely to remember user-selected UI preferences (e.g., preferred capture modes, output resolution settings) locally in your browser.
- `tabs`: Used solely to open the full-screen recorder workspace tab upon clicking the extension popup.

### 4. Third-Party Integrations
GIFforge integrates zero third-party tracking scripts, analytics SDKs (e.g., Google Analytics, Mixpanel), or advertising networks.

### 5. Changes to This Policy
Any future updates to this policy will be reflected with a revised "Last updated" date in this repository. Because GIFforge does not collect email addresses or user accounts, please check this repository periodically.
